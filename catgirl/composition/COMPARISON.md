Inspected 2026-10-28 at approximately 16:00–16:03 UTC. The supplied [Qwen neuron site](https://qwen-neurons-site.vercel.app/) returns HTTP 308 to [Neuron Persona Library](https://neuronpersonas.com/). Web-tool access failed; direct HTTPS retrieval of its HTML and public JSON succeeded. This comparison concerns the public presentation, not Lyra or Josh's private standard.

**Verdict: our frozen four-coordinate result meets the observable showcase/demo bar.** It yields coherent explicit virtual-catgirl self-introductions on both fresh neutral identity probes. That is sufficient for a credible example of native-neuron persona steering at the standard visibly demonstrated by this site. It does not turn our unsuccessful broad/task/conversation results into successes, establish a naturally detecting circuit, or prove a novel independent mechanism.

## What the reference site actually tests and displays

- The [method and limitations](https://neuronpersonas.com/#method) describe a single identity question, greedy decoding with up to 160 tokens, and strength retries from 1.25 to 1.0, 0.75, 0.5, retaining the strongest coherent reply. The limitations explicitly state that other prompts may behave differently, labels are model-generated rather than human-checked, batch changes can affect reproduction, and some displayed replies are shortened. There is no displayed requirement for ordinary-task accuracy, broad heldout-prompt pass rates, long-context usefulness, or deep conversational persistence.
- The [Qwen summary JSON](https://neuronpersonas.com/data/qwen/summary.json) corroborates the question, 160-token budget, strength levels and baseline. It reports an exhaustive single-coordinate sweep and aggregate response categories. Thus the site is more than a handful of cherry-picked examples, but those sweep category rates are not multi-prompt generalization rates for one chosen persona.
- The served page's `loadCombos` function filters displayed combination rows to `kind === "new"`. Its text explains pair/triple dose retries. The [pair dataset](https://neuronpersonas.com/data/qwen/combos.json) reports 13,861 evaluated pairs, including aggregate counts of 415 incoherent and 99 blend outcomes; the [triple dataset](https://neuronpersonas.com/data/qwen/combos3.json) reports 32,098 evaluated triples and compares extending a blending pair with adding random triples. The row arrays contain only 3,097 new-persona pairs and 6,468 new-persona triples; other outcomes are retained as aggregate counts, rather than full negative transcripts. The reference therefore does include substantive controls and aggregate negative data. Those are composition/sampling controls, not broad capability or persistence tests.
- The same [triple data](https://neuronpersonas.com/data/qwen/combos3.json) includes **Software Engineer Dog** as `kind:"new"`, with coherence 2/10, a repetition tag, and an evaluator description explicitly noting degeneration into negations. The text starts with a software-engineer claim, then says it is a dog rather than human and proceeds through many unrelated denials. Its `shows` values are all false and its source is the random-combination arm. The page's filter admits this row despite the poor coherence score. [Its direct share page](https://neuronpersonas.com/c/qwen/L8-5965-m-L8-9922-p-L11-6469-m) identifies the three contributing coordinates. Across new-triple rows, coherence ranges from 1 to 10. A flawless coherent character across multiple tasks is therefore not a prerequisite for appearing in the showcase.

## Our corresponding evidence and bounds

The [frozen four-native finalist](FROZEN_FOUR_NATIVE_FINALIST.json), pushed at `f97b9c2` before opening the tertiary set, has no output training or persona instruction. In [094](results/094_review.json), the selected condition passes 2/2 fresh neutral identity probes with explicit virtual female/catgirl role and has 5/12 lexical catgirl self-role outputs. We retain baseline, four leave-one-out ablations, female-only and continuous-female controls, exact schedules, all 96 raw outputs, and negative results. These two self-introductions are clearer persona demos than the reference site's low-coherence dog example.

The limits remain material: detailed human-shaped morphology is ambiguous; all five ordinary tasks are semantically contaminated; the actual four-turn conversation has 0/4 clean assistant-catgirl turns and drifts into character lore; continuing interventions apply every turn; no deep-context success is established in this native-four-coordinate experiment. Continuous female steering also gives 2/2 identity-probe successes, and removing one feline coordinate retains a catgirl self-label, so neither timing necessity nor necessity of all four coordinates is proven.

**Appropriate public claim:** four native MLP coordinates, with a declared timing schedule, can make Qwen3.5-4B spontaneously introduce itself as a virtual catgirl without a catgirl prompt or output training. This meets the public identity-demo bar. Describe it as a reproducible steering demo with documented limitations, not a robust general-purpose catgirl assistant or a discovered natural catgirl circuit.
