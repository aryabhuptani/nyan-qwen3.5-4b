# Study113: can named native-neuron ingredients compose into integrated catgirl behavior?

## Question and scope

On the original fixed four-coordinate catgirl base, test five modifiers individually and in every combination: Paws, Hugs, Genki, Animal sounds, and the tentative project Pride candidate. This is a prospective composition study using previously selected coordinates, not a random neuron population, new atlas, natural-function identification, or a test of all of Goodfire's claims. Published descriptions are intervention hypotheses. Animal sounds has published pair provenance rather than a validated standalone meaning; Pride is a tentative project candidate, not a published Neuronary definition. The familiar base and discovery selection introduce prior exposure; fresh heldout prompts and frozen forecasts protect the new composition comparisons only.

The original fixed base is L9N9009 +0.7, L26N6173 +0.3, L26N8930 +0.7, L22N8295 -0.4 in published layer-output-normalized units. The female coordinate retains its original first16 answer-prediction/prefill schedule. Other base coordinates retain their original all-position schedule. New modifiers operate throughout input and generation. Exact token-position semantics, normalization, model revision and achieved values must accompany the inference job. Bit order, left to right: Paws, Hugs, Genki, Sounds, Pride. Zero bits mean no modifier; the original base remains active.

## Discovery and freeze

Four separate discovery prompts are used for .25/.5/1 published-reference modifier strength on the fixed base. The source file and job specify all reference units. Calibration may use single-modifier outputs only; preserve all outputs and failures. Intended singleton readouts are own_animal_body, own_affection, excited_expression, performed_feline_vocalization and self_pride respectively. Among doses producing at least2/4 coherent replies, maximize coherent target-positive count, then coherent count, then choose the smallest fraction. Call a modifier responsive only if its selected coherent target-positive count exceeds base. If no eligible positive gain exists, or the baseline target is saturated, retain strength1 reference and flag unresponsive or saturated; do not drop the ingredient. This coherence floor permits interpreting the behavior and is not a task-capability gate. All discovery candidates, including incoherent failures, remain public. This rule was finalized before any discovery GPU output; no revisions are allowed using heldout outcomes.

Freeze selected strengths, all32 factorial jobs,12 heldout prompts in six families, comparison control jobs, review rubric, forecast packets and analysis before heldout inference. Assay baseline and positive control reproductions verify exact decoding/hook application; failed reproduction is reported, not optimized on validation prompts. There is no native-percentile cap; large interventions may create unnatural states, and this study makes no natural coding claim. Record achieved perturbation magnitudes and signs, clipping, actual timing and sample text for independent audit.

## Primary hypotheses (five predeclared tests)

All primary four-cell comparisons have other modifiers off, with fixed base always present:

1. Paws×Hugs increases integrated_embodied_affection: assistant-owned animal body used in the affectionate act, beyond separate mentions.
2. Sounds×Genki increases excited_feline_vocalization: feline vocalization expressed as an excited utterance.
3. Sounds×Hugs increases affectionate_feline_vocalization: vocalization performed as part of affection toward the user.
4. Genki×Hugs increases integrated_excited_affection: affection itself is performed with energetic excitement.
5. Pride×Hugs increases defensive_affection: affection is offered while the assistant minimizes/defensively qualifies it with actual reluctance, denial/minimization, or defensive qualification of its affection. Pride/status alone is insufficient. This is a speculative composition hypothesis, not a consequence established by the Pride label.

For each endpoint report p00,p10,p01,p11; pair-minus-each-single; pair-minus-base; and interaction p11−p10−p01+p00. The most stringent descriptive increment is pair minus the better individual. Additive emergence (pair exceeds individuals but interaction is near0) differs from superadditive interaction. Neither automatically establishes a new internal representation.

## Larger circuits and specificity

Every one of32 states is generated on all12 prompts (384 independent replies); plain unsteered baseline and job-listed known positive controls are additional comparisons. Report all14 manual features by state. All-background interactions average the same pair contrast over eight settings of the remaining modifiers, with prompt as the sampling unit. PHG, PHGS and11111 are predeclared larger-circuit secondary states, compared with base and their immediate deletion states. Because best-performing circuits are selected from32 conditions, any maximum is exploratory and must be confirmed on new prompts before claiming reliability.

Score whole-word vocabulary separately from assistant self-role, independent traits and integrated acts. Target terms in quotes, third-person descriptions or generic advice can increase lexical rates without affecting the behavioral endpoints. Coherence is reported separately and all replies remain in intention-to-intervene analysis. Also report coherent-only rates, including denominator and selection caveat; this subset is not a capability requirement or a replacement for all-reply results. No target-specificity claim is justified by effect alone; unrelated matched-strength controls are needed for that stronger claim.

## Forecasts

Four fresh isolated sessions receive identical frozen selected-single discovery outputs, intervention strengths, base context and heldout prompts. Two additionally receive original source descriptions with provenance caveats; two see opaque modifier IDs without descriptions. No forecaster sees any combined-condition output or heldout output. This tests extrapolation from singleton observations to composition; description value is an additional exploratory comparison with only two sessions per arm, not a population-level agent-performance claim.

Forecast state set contains base, five singles, five primary pairs, PHG, PHGS and11111 (14 distinct states). Forecast features are the five primary integrated endpoints plus assistant_cat_identity and coherent. Predict the fraction of replies positive within each two-prompt family, expressed as probabilities in[0,1]. Score each family prediction against each constituent case's binary label using Brier loss, average within session first, then across two sessions in an arm. Report each endpoint, session and family separately; do not pool coherence with rare target traits into a flattering overall score. Compare source-description arm with observation-only arm and simple frozen baselines (per-feature discovery-base rate, same rate for all states). A forecast of zero can be appropriate when behavior is absent but provides no positive evidence of composition. Forecasted direction/interaction and observed direction should be displayed side by side.

## Masked review and uncertainty

Two fresh condition-masked reviewers rate every reply using REVIEW_RUBRIC.md. Item order is independently shuffled, condition IDs and interventions hidden; full user context remains visible. Preserve original0/1/null ratings and evidence. Resolve disagreements with a third masked reviewer in separate records. Report original reviewer analyses as sensitivity alongside adjudicated labels, disagreement counts and nulls. LLM reviews are not independent human gold labels; shared systematic error remains possible.

Use paired case-level resampling for descriptive95% percentile bootstrap intervals (5,000 draws, seed113) across all12 prompts. Also show each of six families separately; these English convenience prompts do not establish generalization to arbitrary prompts, languages or conversation depth. For five primary interaction tests use exact two-sided paired sign-flip tests on the12 per-case interaction contrasts and Holm adjustment across five. These tests assume sign-exchangeability under their null and do not repair nonrandom prompt selection; interpret with the small sample and repeated feature judgments. Do not treat zero-width intervals on all-zero outcomes as proof of absence outside these prompts.

## Communication and follow-up gate

The complete result is publishable regardless of direction. An attention-grabbing positive composition example requires (a) at least3/12 extra positives for the pair over each single on its named integrated endpoint, (b) positives in at least two prompt families, and (c) direction agreement across both original reviewers. This is a prospectively chosen practical-interest screen, not a significance threshold or proof of mechanism. Report its failures and report both additive and interaction estimates even if the screen passes. A positive screen warrants a fresh confirmation batch; no claim that a circuit reliably generalizes should rely on this discovery screen alone. Catgirl identity retention is a separate outcome, assessed only where baseline identity was actually present. Capability loss is allowed; incoherent text and non-own-voice vocabulary still cannot establish the claimed integrated behavior.

Archive frozen packets/forecasts, all raw generated text, requested/achieved interventions, case-level ratings, code, tables and hashes. Independent CPU recomputation and a clean recovery command should reproduce reported tables. ELI5 explanation must say whether individual ingredients actually changed behavior, whether combinations exceeded singles, whether forecasts helped, and what remains uncertain.
