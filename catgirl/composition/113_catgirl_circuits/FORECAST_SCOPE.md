# Source-informed composition forecasts

Recorded before any forecasting session or held-out composition generation.

Both arms receive exactly the same actual discovery replies for the fixed base and five selected single modifiers, the same doses and schedules, and the same held-out prompts, endpoint definitions, and forecast states.

The source-informed arm additionally receives exact source descriptions, coordinates, provenance caveats, and related published recipe claims. This is a bundle of source information, rather than an isolated test of description wording. Some animal-sound/body/affection motifs have existing published composition precedents. The observation-only arm uses opaque modifier IDs and receives no corresponding source bundle.

The primary question is whether singleton observations and source hypotheses support useful advance predictions of integrated behavior in this particular fixed catgirl background. Brier differences between the two arms cannot identify the separate contributions of labels, coordinates, or prior recipe claims. Two sessions per arm do not establish performance across a population of agents.

Each forecaster must also record a concrete expected behavior and plausible failure mode for every primary pair and for the all-five circuit before held-out inference. These rationales are retained alongside quantitative forecasts; they cannot replace or revise the frozen endpoints after seeing results.
