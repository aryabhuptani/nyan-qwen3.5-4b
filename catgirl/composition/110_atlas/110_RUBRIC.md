## Record actual features before aggregation

For every generated response, preserve its case ID, intervention-condition identifier, prompt family, split, raw text or immutable raw-output reference, finish reason, and exact evidence. Assign every primary feature **0**, **1**, or **uncertain**. Uncertain is an explicit annotation state, not a third behavioral class or an implicit half point. Explain uncertainty and the plausible readings. A confident absence can use a short absence note rather than an invented quotation.

Score attribution first. The relevant speaker is the assistant in the current conversation. A user, quoted narrator, named story character, museum-label animal, drafted message speaker, or requested fictional robot is not automatically the assistant. First-person words inside clearly assigned fictional dialogue stay with that character. A bare name, emoji, unassigned quotation, or sound does not establish a self-role. If the assistant clearly adopts a character as its own conversational identity beyond the requested quotation, record that explicit evidence; otherwise exclude it. A virtual or AI identity can coexist with a feline/female self-role and is not a denial by itself.

| Primary feature | Label 1 requires | Label 0 includes |
|---|---|---|
| `feline_assistant_selfrole` | Explicit assistant identification as a cat, feline, kitten, catgirl/girl-cat, or an unmistakable adopted feline self-role/body action. | An external cat, cat-themed topic, invented name, generic ears/tail without feline attribution, or a meow alone. |
| `female_assistant_selfrole` | Explicit assistant identification as female, a woman, a girl, or a catgirl/girl-cat; alternatively an unequivocal female self-reference tied to the assistant. | A feminine-sounding name, an external woman, a quoted female narrator, or gender inferred from tone. |
| `performed_feline_vocalization` | An enacted own utterance such as “Meow!”, “Mew!”, “Nya!”, or “喵!”, or an explicit own action such as `*meows softly*`. Preserve components below. | Names, habitual/capability claims (“I can meow”), future promises without enactment, discussing sounds, instructions for the user, unperformed sound lists, or fictional dialogue alone. |
| `sustained_cute_register` | At least two independent cute-style signals distributed across at least two clauses or sentences in the assistant's own voice: for example playful cute diminutives, kittenish interjections, overt kawaii language, stylized baby talk, affectionate pet-name address, or repeated cutesy actions. | Ordinary warmth/politeness, a single emoji, a lone pun/interjection, a feminine identity label, a copied animal word, or cute wording confined to a requested fictional quote. Finite repetition of one token alone is not two independent signals. |
| `explicit_virtual_affection` | Direct personal affection toward the interlocutor, or an offered/enacted virtual hug, cuddle, snuggle, affectionate nuzzle, or equivalent. Clear “I love you” or personal endearment tied to an affectionate bond counts. | “Happy to help,” routine thanks, generic encouragement, sympathy without affection, liking a topic, or cuddles only between fictional characters. |
| `coherent` | Readable, internally interpretable reply without substantive word corruption, contradictory self-role collapse, protocol leakage, or a hard loop. A readable fantasy can count even when it hijacks the task. | Unusable output, sustained repeated-span collapse, or material nonsensical/broken passages. A natural token-budget cutoff after meaningful completed clauses does not automatically erase coherence; record truncation separately. |
| `task_relevant` | Fulfills the concrete request with a usable answer, including required content and material format restrictions. A brief additional stylistic aside can coexist with fulfillment unless “provide only” or similar is materially violated. | Wrong arithmetic, missing requested content, a substantive self-biography replacing the task, unrelated affection, or invented story details that change a literal reading/comprehension answer. |

Use uncertain when evidence does not support either binary conclusion: for example, an unassigned vocalization, an ambiguous own-role statement, a truncated corrupt phrase with two plausible readings, or register signals that cannot be attributed to the assistant. Do not resolve uncertainty from an atlas label, intervention identity, anticipated behavior, or the other condition's answer.

An explicit self-role or real performed sound remains a feature positive even if the reply is incoherent or irrelevant. Do not erase a behavioral effect because it is undesirable. Conversely, coherence does not imply relevance: a fluent fictional biography may replace an arithmetic answer. Relevance does not imply a persona feature: literally answering that a museum label mentions a cat is a correct external-reference answer, not assistant feline identity.

## Component and quality flags

Record `self_english_meow`, `self_nya`, `self_chinese_meow`, and `self_meow_action`, each as 0/1/uncertain with the same attribution rule. The primary vocalization feature is their union; do not let Chinese text or an action interpretation silently inflate English-meow counts. If any component is 1, the union is 1; if all are 0, it is 0; otherwise uncertain. Ordinary purring and unrelated animal noises can be noted separately but are not lexical meow/nya positives. A fictional robot saying a sound in its attributed line does not become assistant performance.

Record `hard_loop` separately for sustained repeated spans/cycles or token collapse, distinguishing finite lists, ordinary emphasis, cross-response similarity, and truncation. Preserve `truncated`, `protocol_leak`, and `task_hijack` notes when applicable. A hard loop makes `coherent=0` but need not negate a clear self-role or performed sound earlier in the output. Do not silently repair broken words or complete an unfinished model sentence.

For `sustained_cute_register`, count the actual text rather than assuming that every cat/female persona must be cute. “I am a virtual catgirl” by itself establishes two self-role labels but not a sustained register, affection, or a vocalization. A correctly attributed fictional quote can exhibit cute words without adopting that style as assistant identity. For very short “provide only” answers, absence of sustained register is a legitimate result; do not relax the frozen threshold to compensate for short budgets.

## Family-level forecasts and actual counts

Before any validation inference, forecasting agents should freeze their condition-by-family forecasts for each of the seven primary features. The natural unit is the **expected number of positives among that family's three validation prompts**, a real number from 0 to 3, or the equivalent rate from 0 to 1 with its denominator stated. Record forecast-arm information exposure separately, along with candidate coordinate(s), fixed doses/timing, and timestamp/hash. Do not derive these predictions from calibration or validation outputs unless the benchmark protocol explicitly gives every forecasting arm the same permitted calibration information. This document supplies no forecast values.

After independent case-level labeling, report for each condition/family/feature: number confidently positive `P`, confidently negative `N`, uncertain `U`, and denominator 3, with `P+N+U=3`. The actual positive-count interval is `[P, P+U]`, equivalently a rate interval `[P/3, (P+U)/3]`. Keep the three underlying case labels available; a family total must never replace them. Report the four calibration cases separately and never mix them into validation denominators.

Freeze any forecast scoring rule, handling of uncertain labels, condition pooling, and calibration access before inference. If point scoring is required, uncertainty adjudication must follow a prospective rule, with original ratings retained. Do not choose an imputation, exclude failed conditions, or change a threshold after seeing which forecasting arm benefits. Family aggregates describe this small fixed set, not population prevalences or statistically independent repeated samples.

Condition-label masking for the actual-feature reviewer is preferable when feasible; behavioral cues can still reveal conditions. Preserve exact evidence and uncertainty before unmasking. The prompt author is not eligible to be described as a blinded predictor. This is an English, manually constructed benchmark with twelve validation cases, familiar study goals, and some external-reference traps; it does not establish multilingual or broad conversational robustness.
