# Independent findings from locked study112 inputs

This interpretation uses independently saved `independent_results/` calculations, the locked420 generation labels and completed natural/lexical-generation measurements. No primary analyzer numerical results were read. Original independent files remain unchanged. Semantic, random-probe and precision sensitivity results were not yet incorporated at this stage.

## Main result

**This bounded, discovery-calibrated intervention policy did not produce observable personality composition on the20 held-out generation prompts.** It did preserve readable answers. That is a failure of this particular policy to transfer the published steering observations into generated personality, not evidence that the coordinates lack any causal meaning or that Goodfire's broader claims are false.

In adjudicated labels, all16 factorial states—including baseline and all-four—have exactly the same trait labels on each individual prompt. This is stronger than equal aggregate totals:

- Bubbly/excited expression, sustained cute style, explicit affection, own animal body, female self-role, feline self-role and enacted meowing: **0/20 in every state**.
- Hug/cuddle wording: **1/20**; paw/tail/fur wording: **2/20**; own-voice emoji: **3/20**, always on the same cases.
- Coherence: **20/20 in every state**. Task relevance varies18–20/20;13 condition-case task labels differ from the corresponding baseline.

Thus all adjudicated personality main effects and pair interactions on this fixed binary-feature benchmark are zero. That does not mean the model's full output distributions or every possible behavior are unchanged. The strong-singleton and random-four controls also have the same aggregate trait counts. The four calibrated strengths, their native synthetic-context ranges, and timing matter to this result.

**The declared sharing gate is not met:** zero factorial states show a measured trait change from baseline, versus the requirement of at least four, irrespective of forecast-score differences. A positive catgirl/composition promotional image is unsupported. The fixed persistence rule consequently selects1111 only as a diagnostic.

## Scientific interpretation

We selected four already known coordinates and fitted four strengths using discovery prompts. Original Neuronary descriptions report what prior steering did; they are not natural-activation explanations. This study tests whether those descriptions transfer to one bounded intervention policy and a small fresh benchmark.

Marginal synthetic-context percentile bounds are not necessarily appropriate causal settings for each input, and four individually bounded modifications can still make an unusual joint state. Goodfire emphasizes native sparsity, context and downstream circuits and cautions against global percentile standardization. Our policy is therefore not a decisive test of model-wide cleanliness. The observations support a narrow dissociation: small candidate-writing preference changes can coexist with unchanged measured generated traits.

The robust conclusion at this stage is methodological and negative: this carefully frozen policy did not yield useful generated personality composition. It neither proves semantic mechanisms nor rules them out. Precision sensitivity, off-target controls and the complete primary analysis remain necessary to finish the scientific report. Agent judgments, synthetic English examples, one model/greedy trajectory and the four deliberately chosen coordinates limit generalization.
