# Pre-validation clarifications

Agreed by root and analysis agent after the frozen112 plan, before reading any validation outputs:

- Natural association primary is the **final readout scalar**, matching activation forecast scoring. Standardization uses each coordinate's population SD of final-readout scalars across all60 discovery capture cases. Readout-text mean, carrier mean and keyword-span mean are separate diagnostics. This does not change the generation target range: its quantiles remain discovery readout-text quantiles as frozen.
- Forecasters predict **all four coordinates on all48 natural contexts**, and all four interventions on all12 lexical-writing contexts. Packets contain prompt text and fixed doses but omit case ingredient assignments, semantic labels, keyword-class labels and source metadata that would reveal the correct explanation mapping. Context wording can still provide clues; this is a procedural blinding limitation.
- Lexical matched-ingredient writing contexts are the primary publisher-faithful subset. The full4×4 target-intervention/context-type matrix reports specificity and cross-effects. A uniform increase across all target wordings is not ingredient-specific composition.
- Control matching refers to the fixed discovery RMS scalar-change × down-column norm budget, summed in quadrature across layers for the aggregate. It does not guarantee identical held-out per-position strength, identical downstream effects, or natural-range values for random control channels. Achieved norm ratios and clipping/unchanged frequency must be shown. Quality failure in an unmatched control is not evidence for the target's semantic necessity.
- Yes-versus-No preference shifts, correctness and discrimination are distinct. Increases in Yes responses across both positive and negative contexts do not establish improved semantic interpretation.

These clarifications resolve analysis details prospectively; they do not authorize changing doses, prompts or endpoints after results.
